Christmas Light Installation in Fort Worth, TX

From Cultural District storefronts to master-planned neighborhoods on the west side, we hang, maintain, and take down holiday lighting for Fort Worth homes and businesses all season long.

Built for Fort Worth's mix of homes and businesses

Fort Worth is our biggest market, and it's not one kind of job — historic Westside bungalows, new-build neighborhoods near Chapel Creek and Walsh, and storefronts downtown and in the Stockyards all want something different. We scope each property individually instead of running one template across the whole city.

  • Roofline and eave lighting sized for two-story new-builds and single-story older homes alike
  • Storefront and commercial lighting for businesses downtown, in the Stockyards, and along West 7th
  • HOA-friendly designs for master-planned communities on the west side

FAQ

Do you serve downtown Fort Worth businesses, not just houses?

Yes — storefronts, office entries, and commercial common areas are regular jobs for us. Mention it's commercial when you request an estimate so we can plan the site visit.

How far in advance should Fort Worth homes book?

Fort Worth is our highest-volume market, so it's also where slots go first. Book in October if you want your pick of install dates.
