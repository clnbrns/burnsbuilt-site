Christmas Light Installation in Brock, TX

Brock is acreage country — long driveways, tree-lined entrances, and houses set well back from the road. That's a different job than a standard subdivision lot, and it's one we're set up to quote correctly.

Built for bigger lots

Most Brock properties aren't a quick roofline job — there's a driveway to light, trees to wrap, and often a gate or entrance marker out front before you even get to the house. We walk the whole property during the estimate so the quote reflects the actual job, not a per-linear-foot guess based on a smaller lot.

  • Driveway and entrance-tree lighting for long approaches
  • Gate and fence-line lighting for acreage and ranch-style entrances
  • Full roofline, wreaths, and garland on the house itself

FAQ

We have a long driveway and a lot of acreage — is that a problem?

No — it's a normal Brock job for us. We just need to see the property (or a few photos) during the estimate so we can quote the driveway and entrance lighting accurately.

Do you serve the Brock ISD area specifically?

Yes — Brock is a regular part of our route alongside Weatherford and Aledo.
