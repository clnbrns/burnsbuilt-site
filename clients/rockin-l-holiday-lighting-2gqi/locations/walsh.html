Christmas Light Installation in Walsh, TX

Walsh is one of the fastest-growing master-planned communities in the Fort Worth Metro, and it's right in our backyard — we design displays that fit the community's look, not just the house.

Built for a new-build, HOA-governed community

Walsh's neighborhoods are newer construction with consistent architectural standards, so a holiday display here needs to look intentional, not thrown together. We design around your home's actual roofline and trim rather than a generic strand count, and we're mindful of HOA guidelines on timing and takedown.

  • Roofline and eave lighting sized to new-construction rooflines and trim
  • Wreaths, garland, and entryway lighting that match builder-standard exteriors
  • Flat-rate takedown scheduled around any HOA deadlines

FAQ

Do you work with Walsh's HOA design standards?

Yes — tell us about any HOA rules on timing or takedown when you request your estimate, and we'll design and schedule around them.

Is Walsh a normal part of your route, or an add-on?

It's a normal part of our route alongside Fort Worth and Aledo — not an out-of-the-way trip.
