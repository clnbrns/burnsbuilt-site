Christmas Light Installation in Keller, TX

Keller's neighborhoods are built for a good-looking street — we hang clean, evenly spaced displays that fit the block and come down again the same way in January.

A neighborhood-first approach for Keller

Keller is one of the more established family suburbs in the Fort Worth Metro, and its neighborhoods tend to care about how the whole street looks, not just one house. We design displays that hold up to that — consistent spacing, matched color temperature, and clean lines from the curb.

  • Roofline and eave lighting designed to look consistent from the sidewalk and the street
  • Wreaths, garland, and porch lighting to finish the front door and entryway
  • Takedown and storage so lights are down well before HOA deadlines

FAQ

Do you work with Keller HOA lighting guidelines?

Yes — tell us about any HOA rules for colors, timing, or takedown dates when you request your estimate, and we'll design and schedule around them.

How far is Keller from your other service areas?

Keller sits on the north side of our coverage area — most Keller jobs are scheduled alongside our Fort Worth and Benbrook routes.
