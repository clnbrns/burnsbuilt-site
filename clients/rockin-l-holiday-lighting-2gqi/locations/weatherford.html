Christmas Light Installation in Weatherford, TX

From homes near the historic courthouse square to ranch properties on the edge of town, we hang lighting built to handle Parker County's mix of in-town lots and wide-open acreage.

Square to ranch gate

Weatherford is the Parker County seat, and it has both a historic downtown and a lot of horse and cattle property just outside it. We do both — clean roofline lighting on in-town homes and businesses, and long-run lighting for ranch entrances, fence lines, and driveway trees on bigger properties.

  • Roofline lighting for homes and storefronts near the downtown square
  • Ranch-entrance and fence-line lighting for equestrian and cattle properties
  • Wreaths and garland finished to match historic and modern homes alike

FAQ

Do you light historic or downtown Weatherford properties?

Yes — we're careful with older trim and rooflines and won't use hardware that damages historic siding or brick.

Can you handle ranch entrances and long fence lines?

Yes — this is a regular request in Parker County. Mention your property size and gate location when you request an estimate so we can plan the right amount of lighting and crew time.
