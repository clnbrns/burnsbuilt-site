Christmas Light Installation in Granbury, TX

Granbury's historic downtown square draws visitors all season, and the homes around Lake Granbury want their own show on the water. We hang lighting for both.

Downtown square and lake homes

Granbury is the Hood County seat and sits on Lake Granbury, which makes it a little different from the rest of our service area — the historic square around the courthouse gets real holiday foot traffic, and lake homes want lighting that reads well from the water, not just from the street.

  • Storefront and building lighting for businesses around the historic square
  • Dock and waterline lighting for homes on Lake Granbury
  • Roofline, wreaths, and garland for in-town and lakefront homes alike

FAQ

Do you do the historic square or downtown Granbury businesses?

Yes — we work with downtown property owners directly and coordinate around business hours and any city guidelines for the square.

Can you light lake homes on Lake Granbury, including docks?

Yes — dock and waterline lighting for lake homes is a regular job here, alongside standard roofline work.
